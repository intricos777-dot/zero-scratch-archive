THE HONEST PART
----------------------------------------------------------------
The solo rig is built correctly and verified — the stratum-to-RPC bridge
passes 46 tests, reconstructs the genesis block to the right hash, and
round-trips nBits against Bitcoin Core's own GetCompact across 50,000
values. Three real bugs were found and fixed by those tests, including
a share check that compared the wrong byte order and could never have
passed while the logs looked healthy.

But a correct rig on this hardware still finds a block roughly never:
about 10^21 hashes per Bitcoin block against roughly 10^6 H/s available
on a laptop CPU. You are the only miner against the whole network, so it
is a real lottery with a real prize — just an enormous one. Worth it if
the node is the point; unlikely to pay if the money is.

Pool mining pays fractions of a cent reliably. That was what your
original three scripts actually did, despite the word "solo" in them.
